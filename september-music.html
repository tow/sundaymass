St James 6pm Mass

Choosing the music for September

One entrance, one offertory and one recessional for the whole month. Communion changes each week, keeping one hymn from the Sunday before.

Tap an option to mark it. Choices are kept on this device only — nothing is saved to the planner until someone enters it there.

Already settled — and what is not

All four responsorial psalms are recorded in the planner. The ordinaries are not: August’s set — the Bukas Palad Gloria, the St Louis Jesuits’ Holy Holy, the Mass for Young Americans Kyrie, Amén, Lamb of God and Sing Hallelujah — carries straight across unless someone wants a change, but somebody has to enter it.

6 September already has This is my command to you at the entrance and Make Me a Channel of Your Peace at communion. Both are treated as options below rather than as decisions. Seven slots are open.

What the readings are doing

Year A, Matthew 18 to 21 — the chapters about how a church treats its own. The month runs from correction, through forgiveness, to a generosity that looks unfair, and ends on turning round.

6 Sept23rd Sunday
Ezek 33:7–9 · Ps 95 · Rom 13:8–10 · Mt 18:15–20

Ezekiel is made a watchman, answerable for the neighbour he fails to warn; the gospel gives the steps for putting a wrong right and ends “where two or three are gathered in my name, there am I”. Paul reduces the whole law to one thing: love.

13 Sept24th Sunday
Sir 27:30–28:7 · Ps 103 · Rom 14:7–9 · Mt 18:21–35

Not seven times but seventy-seven, and the servant let off a fortune who will not let off a debt. Mercy received and refused — with the psalm answering, the Lord is kind and merciful.

20 Sept25th Sunday
Isa 55:6–9 · Ps 145 · Phil 1:20–24,27 · Mt 20:1–16

Seek the Lord while he may be found — my thoughts are not your thoughts. The labourers hired at the eleventh hour are paid the same as the first. Generosity that reads as injustice.

27 Sept26th Sunday
Ezek 18:25–28 · Ps 25 · Phil 2:1–11 · Mt 21:28–32

One son says no and goes; the other says yes and does not. Between them stands Paul’s hymn of the Christ who emptied himself. Turning round, and doing the thing rather than saying it.

Entrance — one for the month

Sung four Sundays running, so this is the cheapest place to spend a new song: learn it once, reinforce it three times. Table of Plenty has opened every Mass since 2 August, which makes the start of a month the natural place to change it.

Offertory — one for the month

Philippians 2 on 27 September — he emptied himself, taking the form of a servant — is an offertory text in all but name, and it opens with “have this mind among you which was in Christ Jesus”. Blessed be God has covered the slot since 9 August.

Recessional — one for the month

September ends on doing rather than saying: labourers called into the vineyard at every hour on the 20th, the son who says no and goes on the 27th. A send-off about answering carries the month. City of God has closed most Sundays since 26 July.

Communion — one changes, one carries

Choose the new hymn for each week; the week before’s choice carries into the second slot. All but one option here is already in the repertoire, which leaves the new-song budget for the month spine.

6 September · where two or three are gathered

Carries over from 30 AugustOne Bread, One Body — or We Remember, whichever is worth keeping.

13 September · not seven times, but seventy-seven

Carries over from 6 SeptemberWhichever hymn was chosen above.

20 September · the last will be first

Carries over from 13 SeptemberWhichever hymn was chosen above.

27 September · he changed his mind and went

Carries over from 20 SeptemberWhichever hymn was chosen above.

On the new-song budget. A new hymn in a month-long slot is sung four times for one rehearsal; a new communion hymn costs the same rehearsal and is sung twice. If only one thing is added this month, put it in the entrance or the offertory — unless it is Ubi Caritas, which is four lines long and earns its place on 6 September alone.

Afterwards

Once the choices are made, an authorised editor enters them in the music planner for each Sunday, where they become visible to everyone — including the ordinaries, which still have to be carried over from August. Practice links and copyright details live on the repertoire page.
