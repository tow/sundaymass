St James 6pm Mass

Choosing the music for the rest of August

One entrance, one offertory and one recessional for the whole month. Communion changes each week, keeping one hymn from the Sunday before.

Tap an option to mark it. Choices are kept on this device only — nothing is saved to the planner until someone enters it there.

Already settled

All four Sundays have their ordinaries recorded in the planner — the Bukas Palad Gloria, the St Louis Jesuits’ Holy Holy, the Mass for Young Americans Kyrie, Amén, Lamb of God and Sing Hallelujah — together with their responsorial psalms. Five slots are left to choose.

What the readings are doing

Year A, Matthew. The month runs one arc — faith, then confession, then following — with a strong note of universality in the middle.

9 Aug19th Sunday
1 Kings 19:9,11–13 · Ps 85 · Rom 9:1–5 · Mt 14:22–33

Elijah finds God not in the wind or the earthquake but in a whisper; Peter walks out on the water and sinks. Stillness and storm together — faith holding on when it is frightening.

16 Aug20th Sunday
Isa 56:1,6–7 · Ps 67 · Rom 11:13–32 · Mt 15:21–28

A house of prayer for all peoples, and a Canaanite woman who will not be turned away. The outsider’s stubborn faith, and mercy meant for everyone.

23 Aug21st Sunday
Isa 22:19–23 · Ps 138 · Rom 11:33–36 · Mt 16:13–20

The key of the house of David; Peter’s answer to “who do you say that I am”; the rock the Church is built on. Confession of faith.

30 Aug22nd Sunday
Jer 20:7–9 · Ps 63 · Rom 12:1–2 · Mt 16:21–27

Jeremiah’s fire shut up in his bones; Paul asking for bodies offered as a living sacrifice; the cross taken up. Self-offering, and losing your life to find it.

Entrance — one for the month

Sung four Sundays running, so this is the cheapest place to spend a new song: learn it once, reinforce it three times.

Offertory — one for the month

Romans 12:1 on 30 August asks for bodies offered as a living sacrifice, which is an offertory text in all but name. A self-offering hymn pays off at the end of the month.

Recessional — one for the month

The month keeps pushing outward: all nations on 16 August, proclaiming Christ on 23 August, being sent on 30 August. A mission-shaped send-off carries all four Sundays.

Communion — one changes, one carries

Choose the new hymn for each week; the week before’s choice carries into the second slot. Every option here is already in the repertoire, which leaves the whole new-song budget free for the month spine.

Carries over from 9 AugustWhichever hymn was chosen above.

23 August · you are the Christ

Carries over from 16 AugustWhichever hymn was chosen above.

30 August · take up your cross

Carries over from 23 AugustWhichever hymn was chosen above.

On the new-song budget. A new hymn in a month-long slot is sung four times for one rehearsal; a new communion hymn costs the same rehearsal and is sung twice. If only one thing is added this month, put it in the entrance or the offertory — unless it is You Are Mine on 9 August, which earns its place on the readings alone.

Afterwards

Once the choices are made, an authorised editor enters them in the music planner for each Sunday, where they become visible to everyone. Practice links and copyright details live on the repertoire page.
