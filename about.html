St James 6pm Mass

About & help

A quick guide to choosing a Sunday, recording the music plan and preparing a printable sheet.

About the planner

This is an independent planning aid for the 6pm Sunday Mass at St James the Apostle. Music plans are publicly visible so singers and musicians can prepare from the same information.

It is not an official parish or Diocese of Helsinki publication. Always confirm the liturgical details and proclaimed readings against the parish Ordo.

Planning a Sunday

  1. Choose the Sunday. Use the arrows, date control or “Jump to upcoming Sunday”.
  2. Review the readings. Tap a reading citation to jump to its full text.
  3. Choose the music. Editors sign in, choose from the shared repertoire or add a new song, and may add a YouTube practice link.
  4. Record copyright details. For songs whose lyrics will be projected, fill in the authors, copyright owner or publisher and year. Add a source when it is useful.
  5. Print the plan. Choose either the music sheet alone or music together with the full readings.

Copyright information

Once a song is chosen, its copyright section shows whether the required planning information is complete. Source is optional and does not affect that status. The planner combines the available fields into a consistent attribution line in public viewing and in the printed sheets.

Recording attribution does not itself grant permission to reproduce or project protected lyrics. The church remains responsible for holding the appropriate copying licence or direct permission.

Editing and saving

  • Only authorised editors can change a plan; everyone else can view it.
  • Music changes are live as soon as an editor explicitly chooses, creates, edits or removes a song. Celebration and reading changes save only after their “Use” button is pressed.
  • The rarely needed liturgical controls are behind “Edit celebration or readings” at the bottom of the planner. Prefer “Use another celebration” when the priest celebrates a different standard Mass: it changes the title and complete reading set together while retaining the actual Sunday date. Where the Proper or an applicable Common provides choices, select the actual first reading, psalm, optional second reading and Gospel before saving.
  • Individual reading changes are a secondary fine-tuning option. The planner validates the reading role and previews its full text before saving.
  • A non-standard but valid passage requires confirmation that it has been checked against the parish Ordo or with the celebrant. Use “Restore” to return a slot to its computed reading.
  • There is no separate draft or publish step.

Repertoire and suggestions

Browse the choir repertoire to find songs by title or author and open their recorded YouTube practice links. Browsing is public; song details and private lyrics can be changed only by authorised editors.

When an editor chooses music for a Mass, the picker suggests songs whose recorded lyrics are semantically related to that Mass’s readings. The suggestion index uses Supabase’s built-in English embedding model and private database vectors; lyrics and vectors are never returned to public visitors.

Suggestions are an aid to human judgement, not a liturgical recommendation or a replacement for choosing music appropriate to the part of the Mass.

Calendar and readings

The calendar follows the Catholic Church in Finland (Diocese of Helsinki), including Epiphany on 6 January, St Henry and All Souls. It covers Sunday cycles A–C for 2025–2075 and uses citations from the Order of Readings for Mass.

The full reading texts include visible verse numbers. Always confirm the selected celebration and readings against the parish Ordo. An authorised editor can substitute another celebration from the Sunday lectionary, Proper of Saints or its referenced Commons, then fine-tune individual readings if necessary. The adjusted title, citations and full texts become visible to everyone.

The alternative-celebration catalogue is a planning reference built from public 2002 US lectionary citation indexes plus the St Henry proper. It is not an authoritative or exhaustive Finnish lectionary.

Install on your phone

On iPhone or iPad, open the planner in Safari, tap Share and choose “Add to Home Screen”. On supported Android browsers, use the Install button shown in the planner header.

The installed app keeps the main planner and this help page available for reference when the connection is unavailable. Live plans update again when the device reconnects.
